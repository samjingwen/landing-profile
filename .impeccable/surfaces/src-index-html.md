## Direction contract

THESIS: "Centered Signal" turns Sam's identity into one calm, modern focal point rather than a scattered profile page.
It keeps the directness of the original centered layout while refusing its dated icon-font treatment and loose hierarchy.

OWN-WORLD: Tokyo Night Storm supplies the dark ground, cool ink, and blue-cyan interaction accents.
One raised portrait tile, a tightly grouped identity block, four tactile icon buttons, and a low-density particle field form the component language with restrained bevels and directional depth but without glass, glow, or card clutter.

STORY: A visitor recognizes Sam, reads his role, and chooses LinkedIn, GitHub, email, or The Woke Coder in one viewport.
The portrait and bilingual greeting establish identity while every destination remains explicit and immediately actionable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name leads one centered identity block, with the bilingual greeting and role sharing a supporting line above the portrait.
Four icon-only destinations sit in a centered row beneath the portrait, and sparse particles respond through every foreground region without intercepting it.

FORM: A quiet personal instrument with one centered portrait, compact vertical rhythm, broad negative space, exact typographic alignment, and shallow skeuomorphic depth at the two touchable material layers.
The pinned user direction overrides the dealt visual world while retaining its demand for a complete, functional system.
